# Operations and legal

## The honest boundary

Everything that can be produced without a legal signature has been. What follows cannot be,
and is listed in dependency order with real costs.

**None of this is legal or tax advice.** The templates are drafting starting points for a
lawyer, not substitutes for one.

## The licensing question — read this first

**Business brokerage is a regulated activity in many US states.** Several require a real
estate broker's licence to negotiate the sale of a business or to be compensated on a
transaction; others regulate "business opportunity" sales specifically.

Understudy's scope is drawn to sit entirely outside that:

| We do | We do not |
| --- | --- |
| Assess what a business would likely fetch | Give a certified or USPAP-compliant valuation |
| Coach the owner to make it transferable | Market, list, or advertise the business for sale |
| Prepare a diligence package | Introduce buyers, negotiate, or participate in a transaction |
| Charge fixed fees for work delivered | Take a commission, success fee, or any share of a sale |

**The fixed-fee, no-success-fee structure is the licensing boundary**, and it is also why the
advice can be honest — someone paid on completion cannot reliably say "do not sell yet."

**Step 1 for a human: confirm this with counsel in the state of operation, and in any state
where clients are taken.** Do not rely on this document. The boundary above is designed to
be defensible, but it must be checked, and the answer determines whether marketing language
needs adjusting.

require the partner to confirm their own compliance, and a disclosed-fee variant should exist
for partners whose rules demand it.

## Delivery operations

### The Readiness Report (about 6 hours)

1. **Intake** — three years of tax returns and P&Ls, a completed risk questionnaire, and the
   owner's own view of what it is worth. Capture that last one before showing any number; the
   gap between expectation and appraisal is the most useful thing in the session.
2. **Normalise** — build SDE, itemise every add-back, flag anything undocumentable.
3. **Appraise** — run the engine, sanity-check against recent comparable sales in the trade.
4. **Session (90 minutes)** — deliver in person or on video, never as a document alone. This
   is someone's life's work; a PDF landing in an inbox is the wrong way to say it is worth
   less than they hoped.
5. **Leave the work order** — three gaps, in order, with dollar values and effort.

### The Handover Program (12 months)

Monthly 90-minute working session, assignments between, and a shared operating manual built
as the work proceeds. Milestones at months 3, 6 and 9, with a re-run appraisal at month 12 —
the before-and-after is the proof for the next client.

**Capacity: 12 concurrent programmes per coach.** This is the number that governs the whole
business. It sets the hiring trigger in the model and it is why growth past ~30% conversion
requires people rather than marketing.

### Coach hiring

| Role | Trigger | Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coach 1 | Bench 80% full (~10 concurrent) | Former small-business operator or controller. Operational credibility beats consulting polish |
| Coach 2 | Bench 80% full again | As above |
| Ops / delivery manager | 3 coaches | Scheduling, intake, quality |

The model enforces the 80% trigger rather than hiring on a calendar. The downside case shows
why: operating revenue holds up under halved conversions, but calendar-driven cost does not.

## Data and confidentiality

These clients hand over tax returns, payroll and customer lists. The posture has to be right
from day one.

1. **Written confidentiality in the engagement letter**, surviving termination.
2. **Financial documents retained only as long as the engagement**, purged on request.
3. **No client named publicly without written consent.** Anonymised teardowns are permitted
   only with an opt-out, and never where the business is identifiable from the details.
4. **Never both sides.** No commission, no buyer relationships that could compromise the
   advice, no introducing a client's business to a buyer we have any interest in.

## Cadence

- **Weekly:** partner conversations, scorecards started, reports booked.
- **Monthly:** re-run the operating model against actuals. The report-to-programme conversion
  rate is the headline metric until it is firmly established.
- **Quarterly:** check the stop conditions in `04-go-to-market.md` honestly, and re-run the
  appraisal engine **on this company**, publishing the score.

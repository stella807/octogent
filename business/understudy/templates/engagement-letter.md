## 1. What we are doing

Provider will assess how transferable Client's business is — how readily it could be sold,
financed, or run without its current owner — and will identify, price and help close the
specific gaps that reduce it.

## 2. What this is not

Client acknowledges, and Provider states plainly:

1. **This is not a certified business valuation.** Provider's appraisal is an estimate of a
   likely sale range based on Client's reported figures and market benchmarks. It is not a
   USPAP-compliant appraisal and must not be relied upon for tax, litigation, divorce,
   estate, gift, or financial-reporting purposes, nor presented to any third party as a
   formal valuation.
2. **Provider is not a business broker.** Provider will not market or list the business,
   introduce buyers, negotiate, or participate in any transaction, and is **not compensated
   in any way by reference to a sale.** Provider charges fixed fees for work delivered and
   takes no commission, success fee, or share of proceeds.
3. **Provider is not Client's accountant, attorney or financial adviser**, and nothing here
   is accounting, legal, tax or investment advice. Client should engage those professionals
   independently, and Provider will work alongside them.
4. **No outcome is promised.** Provider does not warrant that the business will sell, sell at
   any price, or achieve any valuation. Whether a business sells depends on markets, buyers,
   financing and Client's own execution — none of which Provider controls.

## 3. Services

**Part A — Readiness Report.** Fee **$1,200**, due on booking.

Provider will: normalise Client's earnings into seller's discretionary earnings, itemising
every add-back; estimate a likely sale range; assess the transferability factors a buyer or
SBA lender examines; assign a dollar value to closing each gap; and deliver the findings in a
session of up to 90 minutes with a written work order.

Delivered within **[15] business days** of Provider receiving everything in Section 4.

**Part B — Handover Program.** Fee **$1,250 per month for 12 months**, billed monthly in
advance by ACH, beginning [date]. *(Elected: ☐ yes ☐ no)*

Monthly working session of up to 90 minutes, assignments between sessions, a shared operating
manual developed through the engagement, milestone reviews at months 3, 6 and 9, and a
re-run appraisal at month 12.

## 4. What Client provides

Client will provide: business tax returns and profit-and-loss statements for the last
**[3]** years; a completed risk questionnaire; reasonable access to the owner for scheduled
sessions; and access to staff or advisors where agreed in advance.

**Provider's work depends entirely on the accuracy of what Client provides.** Provider will
not audit or independently verify it, and inaccurate inputs will produce an inaccurate
appraisal.

## 5. What Client must actually do

Client acknowledges that the Handover Program requires work by Client between sessions, that
Provider cannot perform that work on Client's behalf, and that results depend on Client
completing it. Provider will say so candidly if assignments are not being completed.

## 6. Confidentiality

Provider will keep Client's financial and operational information confidential, disclose it
to no one without written consent, and use it only to perform these services. These
obligations survive termination indefinitely as to financial records.

Provider will retain Client's financial documents only for the duration of the engagement
plus **[90] days**, and will purge them sooner on written request and confirm in writing.

**Provider will not name Client publicly without written consent.** Provider may publish
anonymised, aggregated observations that do not identify Client or make the business
identifiable. *(Client opts out of anonymised publication: ☐)*

## 7. No conflicting interest

Provider will not accept compensation from any party in connection with a sale of Client's
business, will not represent a prospective buyer of it, and will disclose any relationship
that could reasonably be seen to compromise Provider's advice.

## 8. Fees, term and termination

Fees are as stated and exclude taxes. Part A is due on booking and is refundable in full if
Provider has not yet delivered the session.

Part B may be terminated by Client on **30 days'** written notice, with fees due through the
notice period and nothing beyond it. **There is no early-termination charge.** Provider may
terminate on 30 days' notice, refunding any period paid and not delivered.

Provider may suspend work on fees more than [15] days overdue.

## 9. Intellectual property

Client owns its own business information, and the operating manual produced during the
engagement. Provider retains all rights in its methodology, questionnaires, appraisal
software and templates. Provider grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use the
deliverables within Client's business, including providing them to a prospective purchaser.

## 10. Liability

Provider will perform in a professional and workmanlike manner. **Except as stated in this
letter, the services and deliverables are provided without warranty of any kind.**

Provider's total aggregate liability arising from this engagement is limited to the fees
paid by Client in the **12 months** preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for
indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits or lost sale
proceeds. Nothing limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or anything that cannot be
limited by law.

Independent contractors; no partnership, employment, agency or fiduciary relationship.

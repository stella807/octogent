Why most of them never sell

It is almost never profitability. It is provability.

About 45% of deals die on financial issues and more than half collapse during due diligence — not because the business does not make money, but because the owner cannot prove it. Personal spending runs through the books. The process lives in one person's head. The licences are in their own name. Every one of those is a discount, and together they are usually disqualifying.

All of it is fixable, on a schedule, for money. That is the whole of what we do.

What we will not do

  • We do not broker the sale, list the business, or introduce buyers.
  • We take no commission, no success fee, and no share of any sale.
  • We are never on both sides of a transaction.
  • We do not promise a sale, or any price.

Fixed fees for work delivered. Anyone paid on completion will never tell you the business is not ready — and sometimes that is the honest answer.

What lands before any sale

  • You can take two weeks off without the phone ringing.
  • You can hire, because the job can finally be explained.
  • A bank can read your accounts without a translator.
  • If you never sell, you still stop working sixty-hour weeks.

The sale price is the by-product. Most owners come for the holiday.

Where this is weak

Stated plainly, because you would find out anyway.

  • This is not a certified valuation. It estimates a likely sale range and reports a range, never a single number. It is not for tax, divorce, estate or litigation use.
  • It is only as good as the figures you give it. We do not audit anything.
  • Multiples move with geography, timing and buyer type in ways no general model catches. Check ours against recent sales in your trade.
  • Some businesses get no price from us at all. If the books are cash and commingled, no lender will finance a buyer, and we will tell you that instead of quoting a hopeful number.
  • The programme only works if you do the work between sessions. We cannot do it for you, and we will say so if it is not happening.
